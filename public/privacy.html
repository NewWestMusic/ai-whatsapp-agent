Privacy Policy

New West Music · Effective October 8, 2026

New West Music (“we”, “us”) is a music school in New Westminster, British Columbia. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you contact us, including through our WhatsApp assistant, how we use it, and the choices you have. We handle personal information in line with British Columbia’s Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA).

1. Information we collect

When you message us on WhatsApp, or contact us by phone, email or our website forms, we may collect:

  • Contact details: your WhatsApp phone number, WhatsApp profile name, and any name or email address you give us.
  • Student details you share: such as the student’s name and age, the instrument they’re interested in, their level, and your preferred days, times and studio location.
  • Your messages: the content of your conversation with us.

We only ask for what we need to answer your questions and arrange lessons. Please don’t send payment card details or other sensitive information over WhatsApp.

2. Our WhatsApp assistant

Our WhatsApp number is answered first by an automated assistant powered by artificial intelligence (Claude, by Anthropic). It answers common questions about lessons, prices and policies, shares demo booking links, and passes your enquiry to our team when needed. Its answers are generated automatically, so if anything is unclear or important, our office team is happy to confirm it. You can ask to speak with a person at any time.

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to your questions and help you book a demo or register for lessons.
  • To have our team follow up with available times or about something the assistant couldn’t help with.
  • To keep a record of enquiries so we can match students with suitable teachers and times.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising by third parties.

4. Service providers

We use trusted service providers to run our messaging and records. They process information on our behalf and only for these purposes:

  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform): delivers messages between you and us.
  • Anthropic: provides the AI model that writes the assistant’s replies. Under Anthropic’s commercial terms, your conversations are not used to train its models.
  • Airtable: stores enquiry records for our team.
  • Vercel and Upstash: host the assistant and its short-term conversation memory.

Some of these providers store or process information outside Canada, including in the United States, where it may be subject to the laws of that country.

5. How long we keep it

  • Conversation memory: the assistant keeps your recent messages for up to 6 hours so it can follow the conversation, then they are deleted from its memory.
  • Enquiry records: kept for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and any enrolment, and deleted when no longer needed or when you ask us to delete them.
  • WhatsApp itself keeps a copy of the chat on your phone and ours, under WhatsApp’s own policies.

6. Children

Many of our students are children. We expect information about a child to be shared by their parent or guardian. If you believe a child has sent us personal information without a parent’s knowledge, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you,
  • correct anything that’s wrong,
  • delete your information, or stop contacting you.

You can also simply stop messaging us at any time. Typing reset in WhatsApp clears the assistant’s memory of your current conversation.

8. How to delete your data

To have your WhatsApp conversation details and enquiry record deleted, email info@newwestmusic.ca with the subject “Delete my data” and the phone number you messaged us from, or send “Delete my data” to us on WhatsApp. We will confirm once it’s done, normally within 30 days.

9. Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect personal information, including access controls on our records and encrypted connections between our systems. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and limit who can see it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed.
